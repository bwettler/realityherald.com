SCHOLARSHIP · JOURNAL GUIDE

Where Christian legal scholarship can enter broader academic conversation

A curated guide to peer-reviewed journals accepting scholarly work relevant to law, religion, political thought, history, philosophy, and Christian legal argument.

Scope of this guide.

Reality Herald does not list journals merely because they identify as Christian. The guide emphasizes peer-reviewed academic venues with open submission pathways where Christian legal thought—or scholarly responses to legal arguments and legal scholars, whether Christian or non-Christian—may fit the journal’s stated scope.

EDITORIAL NOTE

How journals enter this guide

Reality Herald will favor journals whose current publisher or journal documentation confirms peer review and an active submission route. Listings will describe fit rather than promise acceptance.

Journal policies change. Writers should always verify current author instructions on the journal’s own site before submitting.
