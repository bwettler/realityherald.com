SCHOLARSHIP

Research, journals, and serious academic exchange

A hub for readers and writers who want to move between public intellectual life and formal scholarship without treating Christian scholarship as an isolated world.

Connecting serious readers with serious scholarship

Reality Herald’s Scholarship section will help readers locate academic work, understand current scholarly conversations, identify publishing opportunities, and discover broader academic journals where Christian legal scholarship can enter serious disciplinary conversation.

The initial Journal Guide focuses on peer-reviewed academic venues relevant to theology, history, philosophy, and law, with particular attention to Christian legal thought and scholarly responses to legal arguments.

Find journals that fit your work

Browse journals by discipline, publisher, peer-review status, confessional identity, and submission type. The guide will include explicitly Christian journals as well as broader scholarly publications in which Christian scholars publish.

What the Scholarship section will emphasize

01

Serious venues

Peer-reviewed journals, reputable academic publishers, established conferences, and transparent editorial processes.

02

Intellectual breadth

Christian scholarship appears in explicitly Christian settings and in wider disciplinary conversations. The guide should reflect both.

03

Useful distinctions

Readers should be able to tell whether a journal is confessional, denominational, religiously affiliated, or simply open to scholarship from many perspectives.

04

Practical help

Writers need more than journal names. They need scope, article types, submission links, review expectations, and notes about fit.
