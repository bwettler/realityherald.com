REALITY HERALD PODCAST

Long-form conversations for people who want the argument beneath the headline.

Reality Herald Podcast brings scholars, writers, teachers, lawyers, and careful critics into sustained conversation about law, history, theology, science, culture, education, and first principles.

EDITORIAL PURPOSE

What the show will do

Scholar conversations

Extended interviews with scholars whose work deserves more than a short quotation or headline summary.

Argument analysis

Episodes that identify assumptions, evidence, competing premises, and the point where a dispute actually turns.

Law & cases

Close discussion of major opinions, constitutional disputes, religious liberty, natural law, and legal scholarship.

History & theology

Conversations that connect historical sources, doctrinal disputes, interpretation, and current questions.

Students & teachers

Episodes on debate, research, source evaluation, academic paths, and intellectual formation.

Article follow-ups

Writers and editors revisit major Reality Herald pieces, answer objections, and extend arguments beyond the page.

GUESTS

Who belongs on the show

Scholars

Current researchers whose work connects directly to Reality Herald's core subjects.

Writers & critics

Authors with arguments worth examining carefully, including people who disagree with the publication's contributors.

Lawyers & judges

Guests who can explain legal reasoning, case history, constitutional disputes, and institutional consequences.

Teachers & students

Voices from classrooms, debate programs, academic competitions, and serious independent study.

TRANSCRIPTS & SHOW NOTES

Every episode should remain useful after the audio ends.

Episode pages can carry full transcripts, key sources, cited books and articles, related Reality Herald pieces, guest biographies, corrections, and follow-up material. Each episode becomes part of the publication's research archive rather than disposable audio.
