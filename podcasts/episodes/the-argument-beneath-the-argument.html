Episode summary

Two people can agree on many facts and still reach opposite conclusions. This episode follows the deeper structure of disagreement: which evidence counts, which assumptions connect evidence to conclusions, and which first principles govern judgment.

Questions for the conversation

  • Why do arguments often begin in the middle?
  • How can people agree on evidence yet disagree about what follows?
  • What role do hidden premises play in law, science, history, and theology?
  • How can a disagreement move forward once those premises become visible?
  • Why does recognizing assumptions not require relativism?
