COLLEGES & SCHOLARS

Christian conviction without intellectual sheltering.

This guide does not rank colleges by how Christian, conservative, or comfortable they appear. Reality Herald looks for places where intellectually serious Christian students can encounter first-rate scholars, rigorous disagreement, and sustained academic work that treats Christian or theistic claims as arguments worthy of examination rather than embarrassments to suppress.

CURRENT FACULTY FIRST

Can you actually study with them?

Famous emeriti matter as intellectual lineage, but current professors, centers, courses, and supervision matter more when choosing where to enroll.

NOT A SAFE-SPACE LIST

Challenge counts positively.

A strong entry exposes students to serious atheists, naturalists, liberals, conservatives, Catholics, Protestants, and other competing positions. Intellectual resistance can form better thinkers.

SCHOLARSHIP OVER BRANDING

We follow scholars, not labels.

A university without a confessional identity can rank highly when students can study reasoned study of religion, natural law, moral realism, religious epistemology, or related fields with first-rate faculty.

CURATED DIRECTORY · 20 INSTITUTIONS

Places worth investigating

Faculty and program information was checked against current university pages for the 2026–27 academic year. Students should still verify teaching assignments, leaves, supervision, and course access before making an enrollment decision.

How to read the guide: Exceptional = unusually demanding intellectual environment with major current scholarly strength. Very high = strong scholarly environment and serious exposure to competing positions. High = substantial intellectual opportunity, often with more explicit institutional Christian support.
REASONED STUDY OF RELIGION · LAW · NATURAL LAW

University of Notre Dame

Notre Dame, Indiana, USA

ChallengeExceptional

Notre Dame combines major research strength in reasoned study of religion with unusually deep current work in law, ethics, religious liberty, constitutional structure, and natural-law reasoning.

Scholars to know now

Michael Rea; Laura Frances Callahan; Meghan Sullivan; Richard Cross; Richard Garnett; Sherif Girgis; O. Carter Snead; Jeffrey Pojanowski.

Can you actually study with them?

Students can investigate the Center for Reasoned Study of Religion, the Program on Church, State & Society, and Law, Ethics & Shared Policy offerings.

Emeriti & intellectual lineage

Alvin Plantinga, John Finnis, and Gerard Bradley belong to the intellectual lineage; they should not be confused with the current-study-with list.

Why Reality Herald includes it

One of the clearest examples of Christian intellectual confidence combined with first-rate participation in mainstream intellectual inquiry and law.

WHY A SEPARATE LIST?

A university can deserve mention here even if Reality Herald would not otherwise include the institution in the scholar-driven college guide. Likewise, a church can appear because it runs a serious apologetics class without receiving any implied endorsement of every doctrine, ministry, or institutional practice.

HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE

Do not choose a university because one famous name appears here.

Prospective students should verify whether a scholar currently teaches undergraduates, supervises students in the relevant program, plans to remain at the institution, and offers courses that fit the student's actual degree. Reality Herald will keep expanding this guide while marking emeriti and associated scholars separately from active faculty.
