ASSIGNMENT IDEAS

Use Reality Herald as a starting point, not an answer key.

Find the thesis

Ask students to state an article's thesis in one sentence, identify supporting reasons, and distinguish evidence from assertion.

Find the hidden premise

Have students identify assumptions required for an argument to work but never stated directly by the author.

Build the strongest rebuttal

Require students to construct the best opposing argument before defending their own conclusion.

Compare sources

Give students primary sources, scholarly sources, journalism, and advocacy materials, then ask how genre and purpose affect each source.

Trace a claim

Students follow a factual claim back through citations until they reach the strongest available original source.

Rewrite the argument

Students revise a weak argument by tightening definitions, removing exaggeration, adding evidence, and answering objections.
