Law, justice, rights, authority, conscience, family, political obligation, and Christian legal reasoning.

Classical and contemporary arguments concerning human nature, moral realism, goods, duties, and legal limits.

Free exercise, establishment, conscience, compelled speech, institutional autonomy, and church governance.

Jurisdiction, establishment, incorporation, religious institutions, governmental authority, and constitutional history.
